Generated answer evaluation

The RAG benchmark checks nine answerable questions and six out-of-scope, abstention or validation inputs. It checks legal qualifiers and required citations, including claims that must be supported by different provisions. Playwright exercises a keyboard-operated answer, its expandable quotation and the evidence fallback.

The local Qwen3 4B run passed 10 of 12 cases: four of six answerable cases and six of six abstentions. The complaint answer omitted the authority and the deepfake question fell back to the evidence.

The deployed OpenAI model passed 15 of 15 cases in the latest smoke run: nine cited answers, five abstentions and empty input validation.
